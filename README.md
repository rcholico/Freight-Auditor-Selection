# 🌐 Global Freight Auditor Selection & Requirements Matrix

**Strategic Evaluation of Global Freight Audit & Payment (FAP) Platforms, Governance & AI Data Privacy**

An executive procurement and systems architecture evaluation conducted for a Medical Technologies leader to expand Freight Audit & Payment (FAP) capabilities across North America (AMR) and Europe (EMEA), benchmarking two freight auditing firms.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview & Strategic Context

In North America, the company relies on it's US-based Freight auditor for it's freight spend across 20+ carriers, automating general ledger (GL) coding and AP mass-uploads. As international growth demanded multi-currency, multi-language, and European VAT governance, the company spearheaded a strategic evaluation to select an enterprise-grade global Freight Auditor for EMEA that would later take all the global business.

---

## 📊 Commercial Evaluation & ROI Scenarios

The RFP evaluated a proposed challenger solution across standard and intermediate tiers, comparing projected audit recovery and simulation capabilities against existing the incumbent baselines:

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Incumbent (Baseline)** | USA / North America | $88.0M | $140K – $145K | Baseline Audit & GL Coding | — | Baseline |
| **Challenger (Standard)** | OUS / EMEA | $23.0M | **$51.0K** (£37.5K) | **2.0%** ($460K/yr) | **$1.32M** | **8.6x** |
| **Challenger (Intermediate)** | OUS / EMEA | $23.0M | **$154.0K** | **8.0%** ($1.84M/yr via 50x simulations) | **$5.01M** | **10.8x** |

### Key Vendor Selection Drivers:
1. **Multi-Currency & FX Engine:** Native ingestion of EUR, GBP, CHF, PLN, SEK, NOK, DKK with automated benchmark conversion (ECB feeds).
2. **European VAT & Duty Governance:** Cross-border reverse charges, local customs duty auditing, and EU/UK tax invoice compliance.
3. **No IT Integration Launch:** 12-week implementation model utilizing flat files and automated PDF OCR ingestion without requiring upfront IT resources.

---

## 🔒 Governance, Data Ownership & Final Outcome

Although the challenger demonstrated superior multi-currency, simulation, and EMEA regional connectivity, **the transition did not proceed due to strict data governance constraints**:

* **Data Ownership & Privacy Safeguards:** The company mandated zero-retention of proprietary supply chain data and explicit contractual guarantees prohibiting vendor reuse of customer transport data for third-party AI/LLM model training.
* **Outcome:** The challenger was unwilling to grant full exclusivity and ownership over potential protected data, prompting the company to preserve its data integrity and continue refining the enterprise auditing roadmap.

---

## 🛠️ Competencies & Functional Framework

* **Freight Audit & Payment (FAP) Architecture:** Line-item normalization, accessorial audit, automated GL mapping, and ERP integration (SAP/Oracle).
* **Vendor Procurement & RFP Execution:** Business requirements gathering, cross-functional stakeholder alignment (Logistics, Finance, IT, Legal), and ROI/net-benefit modeling.
* **Global Logistics Governance:** Multi-currency FX management, European VAT compliance, and AI data ownership/GDPR safeguards.
